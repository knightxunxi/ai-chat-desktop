### D2: Methodology Soundness

#### Double Alignment Model
- STRENGTH: Logical chain is valid — team alignment → tool enforcement is a recognized pattern (Conway's Law adjacent)
- GAP: No description of what happens when consensus breaks. How are disputes resolved? Is there a "spec dictator" or democratic process?
- GAP: No measurement of "alignment degree" before vs. after. How did they know alignment was achieved?

#### Technical Debt Mapping
- STRENGTH: "Expert directed + AI scanned" is pragmatically sound — avoids AI noise problem
- GAP: No criteria for "P0 vs P1" classification. What makes a debt P0?
- GAP: No repeatability discussion. Can this be done again by a different lead?

#### Incremental Refactoring
- STRENGTH: "Lead templates → SOP → team parallel" is a proven scaling pattern
- GAP: No failure cases. What happened when AI made errors during migration? How many manual fixes needed?
- GAP: No discussion of regression risk management during 31万-line migration

### D5: Missing Topics (Gaps)

1. **Rollback strategy**: If AI-driven migration breaks production, how to rollback?
2. **Cost metrics**: What was the total AI token cost? Human review hours? Net productivity delta?
3. **Team resistance**: How was buy-in achieved? Any team members pushed back?

## Review Summary

**Article Quality:** Practically valuable as an experience report. Not scientifically rigorous.

**Strongest Contributions:**
1. "人人对齐→人机对齐" framework — translatable to other domains
2. Pre-PR mechanism with AI self-review — actionable workflow improvement
3. Testing SOP with human-in-the-loop — correct direction (avoids full AI autonomy pitfalls)

**Weakest Areas:**
1. No quantitative metrics to support claims
2. No discussion of failure modes or counterexamples
3. Lead dependency not addressed — what if the lead leaves?

**Recommendation:** Accept as practice guide, not as universal methodology. The framework (double alignment, Pre-PR, incremental refactoring) is worth adopting. The specific numbers (90%, 10 bugs, 31万) should be treated as illustrative, not benchmark.

**For C++ developer reader:** The "human-in-the-loop + AI augmentation" pattern applies to any language. The key takeaway is NOT the specific tools, but the philosophy: AI extends reach, human reserves judgment.
